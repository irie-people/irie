# Getting help

1. **Read the docs.** They go from installing Irie to a merged change: [irie-hq.github.io/irie/docs.html](https://irie-hq.github.io/irie/docs.html). [Troubleshooting](https://irie-hq.github.io/irie/troubleshooting.html) lists each stall, failure and message, and what to do about it.
2. **Search the issues.** Someone may have asked already: [irie-hq/irie issues](https://github.com/irie-hq/irie/issues?q=is%3Aissue).
3. **Ask.** Open an issue with the [bug report or idea form](https://github.com/irie-hq/irie/issues/new/choose). Say what you did and what happened, and give what `irie version` prints.

Help happens in public, on GitHub, so the answers can help the next person too. There is no private support channel.

Leave out anything that identifies a person: use `alice`, `/home/alice` and `example.com` in paths, names and logs.

For a security problem, don't open an issue: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Everyone here follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
