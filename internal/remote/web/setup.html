Set up Irie on this iPhone

for your machine

Your iPhone talks to your machine directly over your Wi-Fi, encrypted, and nothing goes through the internet. Irie’s certificate lets your iPhone know it’s really your machine.

Open this page in Safari: only Safari can install the certificate.
1

Install Irie’s certificate

Install the certificate
  1. Tap Allow when Safari asks to download a profile.
  2. Open Settings and tap Profile Downloaded near the top, then Install.
  3. In Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings, turn on Irie local CA.
Waiting for your iPhone to trust it…
2

Open Irie

Come back to Safari once the certificate is on.

Open Irie
3

Add it to your Home Screen

On Irie’s page, tap Share (under ⋯ if you don’t see it), then Add to Home Screen. Open Irie from there and pair it with the code irie remote pair shows on the machine.

Not on your iPhone? Open on it.
