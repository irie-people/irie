# The Constitution of Georgia, 1777: Article LIV

_Article LIV of the Constitution of Georgia, agreed to by the state's convention at Savannah on February 5, 1777, that article alone, as Francis Newton Thorpe printed it in The Federal and State Constitutions, Colonial Charters, and Other Organic Laws (Government Printing Office, 1909), vol. 2, p. 784, in the constitution he titles "Constitution of Georgia—1777": read from the Avalon Project's transcription and settled against two scans of the print's page. Thorpe's source, Watkins's Digest of the Laws of the State of Georgia (1800), p. 15, reads "expence"; the State's Revolutionary Records of the State of Georgia, vol. 1 (1908), p. 296, printed from the State Archives' copy, reads "expence of the state" and ends "point out and direct." Spelling, capitals and punctuation are Thorpe's._

**ART. LIV.** Schools shall be erected in each county, and supported at the general expense of the State, as the legislature shall hereafter point out.
