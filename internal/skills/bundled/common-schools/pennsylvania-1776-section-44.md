# The Constitution of Pennsylvania, 1776: Section 44

_Section 44 of the Plan or Frame of Government in the Constitution that Pennsylvania's convention adopted on September 28, 1776, that section alone, as Francis Newton Thorpe printed it in The Federal and State Constitutions, Colonial Charters, and Other Organic Laws (Government Printing Office, 1909), vol. 5, p. 3091, in the constitution he titles "Constitution of Pennsylvania—1776": read from the Avalon Project's transcription, checked against the Pennsylvania State Archives' transcription, and settled against two scans of the print's page. Spelling, capitals and punctuation are the print's._

**SECT. 44.** A school or schools shall be established in each county by the legislature, for the convenient instruction of youth, with such salaries to the masters paid by the public, as may enable them to instruct youth at low prices: And all useful learning shall be duly encouraged and promoted in one or more universities.
