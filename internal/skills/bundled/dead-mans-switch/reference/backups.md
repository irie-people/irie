# Backups from Google and Apple

Both are free, and both are the user's to set up. Read the page before giving the steps.

## Google: Inactive Account Manager

https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3036546 — set up at https://myaccount.google.com/inactive

- After a waiting period the user picks (Google's own posts say up to 18 months without activity), it can send chosen Google data to up to 10 people, who confirm with a phone number before they can download it.
- It can also set a Gmail auto-reply, and delete the account.
- Activity that counts: signing in, Gmail, My Activity, an Android phone checking in.
- Saved passwords: not confirmed that it includes them (`password-managers.md`).

The steps: open the page, pick the waiting period, add the people (their email address and phone number), choose what each one gets, and write the message they receive. Write in `switch.md` that it is set, and for how long.

## Apple: Legacy Contact

https://support.apple.com/en-us/102631 and https://support.apple.com/en-us/103128

- Needs iOS 15.2, iPadOS 15.2 or macOS 12.1 or later, and two-factor authentication. A Legacy Contact must be at least 13 (it varies by country), and needs no Apple account or device of their own. The user can add more than one.
- Each one gets an **access key**. After the user's death, the contact asks Apple for access with the key and a death certificate. Apple can't replace a lost key, so the user prints it or sends it, and the letter says where it is.
- What they get, for three years: iCloud Photos, Notes, Mail, Contacts, Calendars, Reminders, Messages in iCloud, iCloud Drive, Health, voice memos, Safari bookmarks and iCloud Backup. Not the Keychain's passwords and passkeys, purchases, subscriptions or payment details.

The steps:
- **iPhone:** Settings › [their name] › Sign-In & Security › Legacy Contact › Add Legacy Contact.
- **Mac:** System Settings › [their name] › Sign-In & Security › Legacy Contact.

Then they share the access key with each contact, by Messages or on paper.
