Docs

These pages describe Irie as its code is today: milestones 1 to 3 on the roadmap. Anything still to come is marked Planned, and the mark links to its milestone.

New to all this? How it works explains the ideas in plain words, and the FAQ answers common questions.

  7. Keys and commands

  8. Recipes

  9. Irie at work
