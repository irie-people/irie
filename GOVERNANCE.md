# Governance

How Irie makes decisions, and what happens when the people who run it change. Riddim and Steak, the other Irie People projects, follow the same rules.

## Roles

- **Contributors** are everyone who sends a change, reviews one, reports a bug or helps someone else.
- **Maintainers** look after Irie. They review and merge pull requests, size merged work in Steak, vouch for new contributors and uphold the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Today Irie has one maintainer, its founder.

## How decisions are made

- Decisions are made in public, in issues and pull requests.
- For anything bigger than a small fix, open an issue and agree the approach first.
- A maintainer reviews and merges each pull request. Nobody sizes their own work in Steak, and a change sized 21 needs two maintainers.
- Maintainers decide by consensus: a proposal goes ahead when no maintainer objects. If they can't agree, any maintainer can call a vote in the issue, and a majority of maintainers decides. While there are fewer than three maintainers, the founder breaks a tie.

## Becoming a maintainer

Any maintainer can propose, in a public issue, a contributor who has a record of merged and reviewed work. The contributor becomes a maintainer if no maintainer objects within 14 days. While there is only one maintainer, that maintainer decides.

## When people change

- A maintainer can step down at any time. The other maintainers can stand down one who has been inactive for six months.
- Irie aims to have at least two maintainers with admin rights, so that no one person losing access can stop the project.
- If the founder steps down, or can't be reached for 90 days, the other maintainers carry on and choose among themselves who breaks ties.
- If no maintainer is left, Irie's code stays free under its [Apache 2.0 license](LICENSE), and anyone can carry it on in a fork.

## Money

[STAKE.md](https://github.com/irie-people/stake/blob/main/STAKE.md), in Irie's stake record, sets how any money is shared. Maintainers change it only by a pull request that stays open for 30 days, and Steak gives no vote over it.

## Conduct

The maintainers are the Community Moderators named in the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). A maintainer involved in a report takes no part in deciding it.

## Changing this document

A change to how decisions are made stays open as a pull request for at least 14 days, and needs the approval of a majority of maintainers.
